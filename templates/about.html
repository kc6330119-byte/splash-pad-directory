About Splash Pad Locator

Our Mission

Splash Pad Locator is dedicated to helping families find the best splash pads, spray parks, and water play areas across the United States. We believe every family deserves easy access to safe, fun, and affordable outdoor water play — especially during summer.

Whether you're a parent looking for a free splash pad for a toddler, or a family planning a day trip, our directory makes it easy to find exactly what you need — with hours, photos, admission prices, and amenities all in one place.

What We List

  • 💧
    Splash Pads — Ground-level water jets, spray nozzles, and dumping features at parks and rec centers
  • 🌊
    Spray Parks — Larger spray areas with multiple water features, often in dedicated parks
  • 🏠
    Indoor Water Play — Year-round indoor water play areas, great for colder months
  • 🛝
    Water Playgrounds — Playgrounds with integrated water features
