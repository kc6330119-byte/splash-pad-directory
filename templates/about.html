Our Story

Splash Pad Locator was built by a parent who got tired of the same frustrating experience: searching online for a splash pad near a new city, finding outdated blog posts, broken links, and listings with no hours, no photos, and no indication of whether the place was even still open.

The idea was simple — build the resource that should have existed all along. A single, searchable directory covering every splash pad, spray park, aquatic center, and water play area in the United States, with real information that helps families actually plan a visit.

Today, Splash Pad Locator is one of the most comprehensive water play directories in the country, with over {{ total_count | default('2,900') }} locations across all 50 states — and growing every week.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make water play accessible to every family in America. Splash pads and spray parks are one of the few truly free, all-ages outdoor activities available in most communities — but only if families can find them.

We believe no family should miss a great splash pad simply because it wasn't easy to find. Whether you're a local parent looking for something new, or a family on a road trip searching for a place to cool off, Splash Pad Locator is here to help.

What We List

Our directory covers the full spectrum of water play — from small neighborhood spray pads operated by city parks departments to large regional water parks and indoor aquatic centers.

  • 💧
    Splash Pads & Spray Parks — Ground-level water jets, spray nozzles, and interactive water features at parks and recreation centers. Most are free and open seasonally.
  • 🌊
    Water Parks & Aquatic Centers — Larger facilities with slides, wave pools, lazy rivers, and admission fees. Great for a full-day family outing.
  • 🏠
    Indoor Water Play — Year-round indoor water play areas inside recreation centers, resort water parks, and family entertainment venues.
  • 🎢
    Amusement & Theme Parks — Major parks with dedicated water play areas and splash zones alongside their other attractions.
